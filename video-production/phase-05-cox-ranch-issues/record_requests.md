# Cox Ranch — records request and verification checklist

This checklist prioritizes primary records needed to close the open questions in the [issue matrix](issue_matrix.md). A record not located in this review is **not** evidence that it does not exist or that a legal requirement was violated.

## Priority 1 — establish what the Council actually did on September 28

- [ ] **Certified City Clerk minutes and journal** for the September 28, 2026 City Council meeting, including the complete roll call on agenda items 6, 7, 8, and 23; motion language; ordinance/resolution numbers; and each member’s vote.
- [ ] **Executed, final copies** of the annexation ordinance, assigned-zoning ordinance, BP zoning ordinance, and PlanCheyenne/Future Land Use Map–Urban Service Boundary resolution, including all signatures, final exhibits/maps/legal descriptions, and the effective-date clauses.
- [ ] Every **substitute, amendment, revision, exhibit, or late agenda supplement** considered or adopted at first, second, or third reading; identify which version passed at each reading and attach the clerk’s file date.
- [ ] **Proof of post-adoption publication** for each ordinance/resolution where required: complete published text or summary, newspaper name, publication dates, publisher affidavit, and any clerk certificate.
- [ ] **Recording/filing receipts** for each final legal description and map, including the Laramie County Clerk/Recorder instrument number, date, and exact recorded plat/map.
- [ ] Confirm with the Clerk whether the September 28 minutes have been prepared or posted since the October 2 archive check; request the date they were approved and the official link.

## Priority 2 — reconcile the parcel area and contiguity geometry

- [ ] Final, **signed and recorded** annexation map and metes-and-bounds description in editable/vector form, with acreage computation, scale, north arrow, and coordinate reference system.
- [ ] The City’s source GIS/CAD layers and workpapers showing: (a) the City boundary immediately before the vote, (b) each annexation polygon, (c) existing streets/right-of-way, (d) ownership parcels, and (e) any City-owned/noncontiguous tracts.
- [ ] A parcel-by-parcel acreage and ownership schedule as of the petition-certification and Council-action dates. Identify whether/how the calculation excludes streets, alleys, and tax-exempt property.
- [ ] The calculation behind **5.26%**: exact contact length, total perimeter used, boundary version/date, treatment of right-of-way and intervening features, and which parcel(s) it measures.
- [ ] Written reconciliation of the packet figures: **1,193.4**, **1,197.74**, **1,197.76**, **1,252.09**, **1,259.91**, **10.23**, and **62.21** acres. State which footprint each number refers to; do not assume they describe one identical polygon.
- [ ] City’s analysis, legal opinion, or staff memorandum for the **approximately 194-acre / 15% alternative** and any staged annexation concept, including the underlying map and legal/statutory basis.
- [ ] For every claimed noncontiguous City parcel, the exact deed/assessor record, ownership date, current statutory subsection used, map, and written finding explaining the basis for including it under § 15-1-407.

## Priority 3 — verify the petition and required findings

- [ ] Clerk’s written **certification of petition compliance**, certification date, all calculations and source records used, and any request to cure deficiencies or corrected/replacement pages.
- [ ] Petition denominator worksheet identifying each owner and parcel; address co-ownership, entity ownership, tax-exempt property, streets, alleys, City land, and any parcel added/removed between filing and certification.
- [ ] Final Council **written findings** under each applicable subsection of § 15-1-402(a), together with the evidence and exhibits cited for services, safety/welfare, logical/feasible extension, contiguity/adjacency, and notice.
- [ ] Water/wastewater capacity and extension materials, utility letters, service plans/cost estimates, fire/police access analysis, and any intergovernmental agreement tied to the final boundary.

> Please provide the certified September 28, 2026 City Council minutes/journal and roll-call record for agenda items 6, 7, 8, and 23; every adopted motion, amendment, substitute, signed final ordinance/resolution and exhibit; ordinance/resolution numbers; effective-date and publication records; and the final recorded annexation legal description and map. For the larger production, request electronic/native files where available and ask the City to identify the custodian, date range, search terms, and withheld/redacted item for each category. Keep the produced file names, date received, source URL, and any checksum alongside the working copy so later figures can be traced to the exact version.
