+A strong cold front will sweep through the area on Saturday morning, bringing colder temperatures and possible snow.
+Saturday’s snowfall may be locally heavy, resulting in reduced visibility and minor accumulations impacting holiday travel.
+At a 50% chance, another round of strong winds is possible in wind-prone areas Sunday through early Tuesday.
+Friday will remain mostly sunny, with temperatures falling to around 50 degrees by 5 p.m. It will also be windy, with a west-northwest wind at 30-35 mph and gusts as high as 55 mph.
+There’s a 20% chance of rain after 11 p.m. Skies will be mostly cloudy, accompanying a low of around 34 degrees. It will be breezy, with a west wind of around 25 mph and gusts as high as 45 mph.
+On Saturday, rain is likely before 8 a.m., followed by rain and snow until 11 a.m. Snow is likely after that. Saturday will be partly sunny, with temps rising to near 42 degrees by 9 a.m., then falling to around 28 degrees for the remainder of the day. It will be windy as well, with a west wind at 25–30 mph shifting to the north in the afternoon. Winds could gust as high as 50 mph. The chance of precipitation is 70% and new snow accumulation of less than a half-inch is possible.
