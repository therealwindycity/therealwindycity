High-speed winds complicate travel around Cheyenne today

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Strong, gusty winds are causing some travel restrictions in Cheyenne today.
The Wyoming Department of Transportation is reporting
extreme blowover risks on Interstate 25 between Cheyenne and Exit 29; on Cheyenne Service Road between Exit 335 and Exit 359; and on U.S. Highway 85 between Cheyenne and Meridan. Some roads are closed to all high-profile vehicles under 25,000 pounds this morning.
A
high wind warning
is in effect until 5 p.m. for Laramie County, according to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne. West winds have been reaching speeds of 30–40 mph, with gusts reaching 60 mph. In wind-prone areas along the interstates, gusts could reach 60–75 mph.
Strong winds will continue through Wednesday morning across much of southeast Wyoming, spreading eastward into western Nebraska. Winds will begin to taper off during the afternoon, dropping off quickly after sunset. (National Weather Service graphic)
Relative humidity has dropped to between 14% and 20% over the High Plains. Because of the dry fuels and wind speeds, meteorologists upgraded a fire weather watch to a
red flag warning
from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. for southeast Wyoming and parts of western Nebraska. Outdoor burning is not recommended, as any fires that start will spread quickly.
Today’s skies in Cheyenne will be sunny with a high near 46 degrees. Winds will ease to 20–30 mph later this afternoon and die down quickly this evening. Tonight will be partly cloudy with a low near 24.
Calmer weather returns Thursday. Cheyenne will see partly sunny skies with a high near 51. Winds will remain light, though fire danger will stay elevated near the Laramie Range. Thursday night will be mostly cloudy before gradually clearing, with a low around 27.
Friday will be sunny and breezy with a high near 52. West winds will pick up to 20–25 mph in the afternoon, with gusts of up to 40 mph. Friday night brings partly cloudy skies and a low around 24.
A cold front will drop temperatures for the weekend, bringing a 20% chance of snow showers before 11 a.m. Saturday. Saturday will be mostly sunny with a high near 41 and an overnight low near 19.
Sunday will be mostly sunny with a high near 45 and a low near 21.
Detailed Forecast
Today:
Sunny, with a high near 46. Windy, with a west wind at 30–40 mph decreasing to 20–30 mph in the afternoon. Winds could gust as high as 60 mph.
Tonight:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 24. West-southwest wind at 5–10 mph.
Thursday:
Partly sunny, with a high near 51. Calm wind shifting to the south-southeast at around 5 mph in the afternoon.
Thursday Night:
Mostly cloudy, then gradually becoming mostly clear, with a low around 27. West-southwest wind at around 5 mph.
Friday:
Sunny, with a high near 52. Breezy, with a west wind at 10–15 mph increasing to 20–25 mph in the afternoon. Winds could gust as high as 40 mph.
Friday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 24. Breezy.
Saturday:
A 20% chance of snow showers before 11 a.m. Mostly sunny, with a high near 41.
Saturday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 19.
Sunday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 45.
Sunday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 21.
Monday:
Partly sunny, with a high near 41.
Monday Night:
A chance of snow showers. Mostly cloudy, with a low around 20.
Tuesday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 41.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
