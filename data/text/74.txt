Cheyenne Municipal Building June 15, 2026 (Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — All city offices and public facilities will be closed for Thanksgiving, including transit and waste pickup services. A majority of those facilities will remain closed through Black Friday as well.
All main city offices will be closed both Nov. 26 and 27, the City of Cheyenne confirmed.
The Compost Facility
will be closed Nov. 26 and 27. The Sanitation Division office will be closed in line with other city offices on Thursday and Friday.
Unlike the Compost Facility, the
Happy Jack Landfill
and
Solid Waste Transfer Station
will be closed on Thanksgiving but will reopen on Black Friday.
Residential trash and recycling pickups that are scheduled for Thursday will move to Friday, Nov. 27. Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday pickups will come as scheduled.
Buses will not run on Thanksgiving or Black Friday, and the Cheyenne Transit Program office will be closed both days.
The Cheyenne Aquatic Center and BEAST Facility will also be closed both days. Other community recreation facilities, such as Cheyenne’s two public golf courses and the Cheyenne Ice and Events Center, will be closed on Thanksgiving and reopen on Black Friday.
The Cheyenne Botanic Gardens will be closed starting even earlier. The facility will close for maintenance Sunday, Nov. 22 and stay closed through the holiday. It will reopen Saturday, Nov. 28 at 10 a.m. and resume its normal Tuesday–Saturday schedule thereafter.
See the City of Cheyenne website
here
for more information.
