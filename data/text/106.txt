Chilly temps, morning freezing fog to start Thanksgiving week in Cheyenne
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Nippy temperatures and patchy freezing fog are kicking off Thanksgiving week in the capital city.
Today will be mostly cloudy with patchy freezing fog before 8 a.m. and a high near 39 degrees, according to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne. A light breeze is expected from the east-southeast this afternoon. Tonight will see mostly cloudy skies and a low around 17, with another round of spotty freezing fog possible into Tuesday morning.
Areas of locally dense freezing fog have developed and will persist through the morning. Otherwise, expect mostly cloudy skies and chilly temperatures for the rest of the day today. (National Weather Service graphic)
Sun and warmer temperatures return Tuesday, sending the mercury to a high near 46 alongside a west wind of 10–15 mph. Tuesday night will be mostly clear, with a low around 24.
The warming trend continues Wednesday amid sunny skies and a high near 48. The overnight low will be around 23. Thanksgiving Day is expected to be mostly sunny and pleasant, with highs climbing into the low to mid-50s.
Strong winds are expected in wind-prone areas Thursday night and will spread into the High Plains on Friday, when Cheyenne will be mostly sunny and windy with a high near 53.
Slight chances for rain and snow return Friday night into Saturday, with highs in the low 50s and overnight lows in the upper 20s and 30s. However, meteorologists said in forecast discussion that models are evenly split on the weekend outlook, meaning temperatures could either stay in the 50s and low 60s or drop into the 20s and 30s with snow showers.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
