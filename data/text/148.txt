Patchy fog expected in Laramie County ahead of warm, sunny midweek

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Early-morning commuters in Laramie County may run into patchy freezing fog through sunrise today.
The National Weather Service in Cheyenne said drier air riding in on winds from the west will help ease foggy conditions, leading to sunshine and a high of 48 degrees this afternoon. Winds are expected to remain relatively light at 10–15 mph, dropping to 5–10 mph overnight. Cheyenne’s skies will be clear tonight as temps drop to a low of 23.
Areas of patchy freezing fog are expected in parts of Laramie County on Tuesday. (National Weather Service graphic)
Ahead of potential snow Friday evening, the capital city is expected to continue seeing sunny skies. Wednesday will see plenty of sunshine with a high of 49 as west winds shift to come from the south. Some clouds will build overnight as the low drops to 23.
Thanksgiving is the warmest day of the week, with the mercury climbing to 56 under sunny skies. Winds from the west will remain light at 5–10 mph into the evening as the low falls to around 33.
The next system will approach southeast Wyoming on Friday, bringing strong winds and the chance for rain and snow after sunset. After daytime temps hit a high of 55, Cheyenne will fall to a low of around 32 degrees. Precipitation chances pick up after 11 p.m. and continue through Saturday. Saturday will see a high near 50 before a low of 23 overnight.
Mostly sunny skies are expected to return Sunday with a high near 47, followed by a high Monday of 53. Overnight lows will return to the mid-20s.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
