Cheyenne Happenings (11/20, 11/21): Christmas Craft Show, Holiday Night Market, more

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The weekend is almost upon us, which means you made it through another week. You should be proud of yourself, and you should treat yourself to the myriad of activities happening throughout Cheyenne this weekend. From a Christmas Craft Show to a Holiday Night Market, this is what’s happening this weekend in Cheyenne.
Story Time at Cheyenne Botanic Gardens
Paul Smith Children’s Village is hosting a Story Time event at the Cheyenne Botanic Gardens on Friday, beginning at 11 a.m.
“Join us Fridays at 11 a.m. for a story, a fun project, and shenanigans,”
the event page states
. “Meet under the blue dome during the summer. During the winter, meet us in the classroom in the Children’s Village.”
Happening Friday at 11 a.m.
Book Babies
On Friday, the Laramie County Library is hosting Book Babies from 9:15 to 10 a.m.
“Book Babies is a shorter, less-structured early literacy class just for babies (best for ages 0–24 months)!”
the event page states
. “It’s a great time to bond with your baby and inspire a love of reading. You will learn lap bounces, tickle rhymes, movement songs, and fingerplays that you can do with your baby at home, too! We will typically read one developmentally appropriate book and explore one flannel story each week. It’s also a great time for babies to start learning how to socialize with other children. After the stories, we have Stay and Play where babies can play with developmentally appropriate toys while caregivers visit.”
Happening Friday at 9:15 a.m.
‘Antigone’ by Sophocles
On Friday, Laramie County Community College is hosting its production of Antigone, beginning at 7 p.m.
“A college theater production of Sophocles’ classic tragedy at Laramie County Community College,”
the event page states
. “The play runs for two weekends, presenting ancient Greek drama in an academic performance setting.”
Happening Friday at 7 p.m.
Country Christmas Craft Show
On Saturday, the M&K Building at the Archer Complex is hosting the Country Christmas Craft Show from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.
“Handmade gifts. Unique holiday items. One-of-a-kind finds,”
the event page states
.
Happening Saturday at 9 a.m.
Yoga Together
On Saturday, the Laramie County Library is hosting Yoga Together for kiddos, beginning at 10:15 a.m.
“Come and join Jasmine Wilcox from GROW Movement and Mindfulness for a special literacy class filled with songs, stories, stretching, and play,”
the event page states
. “This early literacy class is best for children ages 3–6 years old.”
Happening Saturday at 10:15 a.m.
Build Your Own Fall Floral Arrangement at Black Tooth Brewery
On Saturday, Black Tooth Brewery is hosting as Build Your Own Fall Floral Arrangement class, beginning at 11 a.m.
“Join us for a self guided fall floral arrangement session,”
the event page states
. “All materials will be provided including florals, vases, ribbons, and of course one drink of your choice. Tickets are $40 and can be purchased in the taproom.”
Happening Saturday at 11 a.m.
Tails for Tales
On Saturday, the Paul Smith Children’s Village is hosting Tails for Tales, beginning at 11 a.m.
“Join us at the Paul Smith Children’s Village for a special story time with certified therapy dogs,”
the event page states
. “Enjoy a good book, meet some furry friends, and create a fun dog-themed project to take home! It’s going to be a paws-itively wonderful time with Tails For Tales Reading Buddies.”
Happening Saturday at 11 a.m.
The Spirit of Christmas Awaits
On Saturday, the Cheyenne Depot Plaza is hosting a Christmas Celebration, beginning at 5:30 p.m.
“There’s something special about Christmas in downtown Cheyenne, where a sense of mystery and wonder fills the air with anticipation for the holiday journey yet to come,”
the event page states
. “Take a stroll along 17th Street and explore Downtown First’s first-ever Holiday Night Market. Don’t miss Visit Cheyenne’s wreath laying procession, Lighting the Legacy, down Capitol Avenue led by the Cheyenne Frontier Days Mounted Marshals. Check out their social media page for more information! Join us as we gather at the historic Cheyenne Depot to illuminate the heart of downtown and welcome another extraordinary holiday season. Together, we’ll celebrate the traditions, memories, and moments that make Christmas in Cheyenne so special.”
Happening Saturday at 5:30 p.m.
November Dueling Pianos
On Saturday, The Metropolitan Downtown is hosting November Dueling Pianos, beginning at 7 p.m.
“A request-driven dueling pianos show where the audience picks the songs and can sing along. Dinner reservations beforehand are recommended,”
the event page states
.
Happening Saturday at 7 p.m.
