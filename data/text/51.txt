Rosemary A. Kylander
Rosemary A. Kylander:
1950 – 2020
On Saturday, June 13, 2020, Rosie Kylander passed away peacefully at her home.
Rosemary “Rosie” (O’Brien) Kylander was born on November 2, 1950, in Cheyenne, Wyoming to Philip and Julia (Hall) O’Brien. She graduated from St. Mary’s Catholic High School in 1969, and she received her Associate of Arts and Sciences Degree in 1971 from Laramie County Community College. She married Jed Kylander in 1973 and moved to Longmont, Colorado where their children were all born: Rozann in 1974, Jed in 1978, and Jared in 1980. Rosie and Jed moved back to Cheyenne in 1993.
Rosie loved being a mom and spending time with her family. She enjoyed traveling and taking family road trips with the camp trailer every summer.
Rosie provided in-home daycare for 20+ years in both Longmont and Cheyenne. It gave her much joy watching all her different babies grow and being a part of so many families’ lives.
Rosie had a strong faith, which helped guide her in her daily life.
Rosie was a voracious reader and was a member of book clubs in both Longmont and Cheyenne. She always had a book started and more waiting to be read.
Rosie loved music-mostly rock and roll. It started with the Beatles and, more recently, her favorites were Aerosmith and Bon Jovi.
Rosie was preceded in death by her parents, Philip and Julia O’Brien; and her sister-in-law, Valerie O’Brien.
She is survived by her husband of 47 years, Jed Kylander; her daughter, Rozann Goodrich (Chad Diesing) and grandsons Kaycee and Brody; her son, Jed (Teri) Kylander and grandchildren Ella and Evan; her son, Jared (Amber) Kylander and grandchildren Kash and McKinlee; her brother, Bill O’Brien; niece, Brittany Lozano and her children, Caden and Nikki; and sister-in-law, Sue (Dennis) Pratt.
Services will be held on Friday, July 17, 2020 at 2 p.m. at the Wiederspahn-Radomsky Chapel with a reception to follow at The Gathering Place. The service will be streamed live at wrcfuneral.com. In lieu of flowers, the family requests that donations be directed to Alzheimer’s Disease Research: brightfocus.org/stopAD.
Gary L Sharp:
1937 – 2026
Gary L Sharp, 89, passed away peacefully on November 18, 2026, in Cheyenne, Wyoming, following a multi-year struggle with a variety of medical issues. He was born on March 22, 1937.
Gary built a life marked by learning, service, and steady dedication to his work. He studied at LIFE Bible College, earned his bachelor’s degree from Cal State Los Angeles, his Master of Business Administration from Cal State Dominguez Hills, and his Master of Public Administration from the University of Southern California. He went on to serve as a College Professor of Computer Science at Laramie County Community College. Outside of his professional life, he enjoyed fishing, bicycling, running, tennis, and exercising.
He is survived by his devoted wife of 40 years, Denise Sharp; his son, Greg Sharp and Greg’s wife, Lori Sharp; son, Brad Sharp and Brad’s wife, Karen and children, Sydney and Cameron; and his stepdaughter, Sara McCarthy, her husband Adam, and their daughters Rachel, Kara, and PJ.
He was preceded in death by his father, Carroll Sharp; his mother, Fern Sharp; his sister, Judy Sharp; and his brother, Charles Sharp.
Ronald Adalbert Thiel:
1934 – 2026
Ronald Thiel, 92, passed away at home on November 17, 2026. He was born on October 28, 1934, at his grandmother’s home in Glen Ullin, North Dakota.
Ronald attended school in Center and Carson, North Dakota, graduating from high school in 1952. He continued his education at North Dakota State University, Montana State University, and Northern Montana College. In 1954, he entered the United States Army and served in Germany.
Ronald was a lifelong devout Lutheran and a life member of the Elks, American Legion, and National Bison Association. He also served as a board member of the Bismarck Junior College Lineman School.
On September 7, 1957, Ronald married Janice C. Helt. They shared 58 years of marriage. He loved Janice very much, and their years together were a cherished part of his life.
Throughout his career, Ronald spent 39 years running an electrical transmission powerline construction company and was a pioneer in the commercial bison industry, introducing several innovative practices. Among his accomplishments were holding the first private, on-ranch bison sale in America, introducing ultrasound as a performance tool, successfully artificially inseminating bison cows on a large scale, and building America’s first bison-only feedlot.
Ronald believed deeply in his faith and the grace of God. Among the beliefs he held close were that hard work does not get you to heaven, but rather the grace of God, and the words, “Judge not, lest ye be judged.”
Ronald was preceded in death by his beloved wife, Janice; his parents, Mike and Gertrude Thiel; his brothers, Dennis and Douglas Thiel; his daughter, René Thiel; and his grandson, David Lenhardt.
He is survived by his children: Michael Thiel (Kristen), Daniel Thiel, and Amy Lenhardt (Kent); his 10 grandchildren: Jon Thiel (Megan), Elizabeth Seilstad (Cory), Kimberly Thiel, Casey Thiel (Lindsey), Rebecca Sod (Graham), Danielle Thiel, Emily Thiel, Jordan Caudle (Domique), Steven Lenhardt (Aubrie), and Melanie Lenhardt; and 13 great-grandchildren; and also his care giver for the last three months, Cathy Potosnyak.
Ronald leaves behind a legacy of hard work, innovation, faith, and family. His contributions to the industries he helped shape, his devotion to his loved ones, and the values he lived by will be remembered by those who knew him.
In lieu of flowers, the family asks that you donate to the charity of your choice.
