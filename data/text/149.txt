All-City Children’s Chorus to present ‘Branches in Bloom’ Christmas concert

CHEYENNE, Wyo. –– Cheyenne’s All-City Children’s Chorus has been preparing for weeks as it heads into its busy Christmas performance season.
“Our last concert was in the first week of October, so we started on our Christmas music the following week,” said artistic director Janet Weisbrook. The music for this year’s program, which takes place on Saturday, Dec. 5, was selected in the summer.
Along with its own concert, the group is preparing to perform with the Cheyenne Symphony Orchestra for its holiday concert on Dec. 12. They’ll be joined by the Cheyenne Chamber Singers, the Capital Choral and the En Avant dance group for that performance, Weisbrook said.
Members of the Cheyenne All-City Children’s Chorus are seen during rehearsal in a contributed photo. (Courtesy Cheyenne All-City Children’s Chorus)
The group has two choir ensembles, she said. Weisbrook directs the 44-member Concert Choir consisting of fifth through eighth graders. The younger students in the Lyric Choir will perform at the group’s concert on Dec. 5, but not with the symphony, she said.
The group celebrated its 50th anniversary last season, and this year has been a simpler “back-to-basics” approach, she said. “I’m kind of excited; one of our arrangements that we’re doing is ‘We Need a Little Christmas,’ but it’s got a Latin flavor to it and some really cool rhythms we’re working on.”
They’ve also prepared a sprawling medley of Christmas carols, she said. “I think our audiences are really going to love it because it’s got a lot of traditional carols and some different arrangements.”
The concert is Saturday, Dec. 5 at 4 p.m. at Cheyenne South High School, with no charge to the public.
The group holds auditions each spring. More information can be
