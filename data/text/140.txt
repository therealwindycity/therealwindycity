What is answer engine optimization?
Answer engine optimization is the practice of preparing content so that AI assistants and search engines can retrieve it, understand it and cite it when they answer a question. It is also called AI search optimization or generative engine optimization, and it overlaps with traditional search engine optimization. AEO explains the overall approach in its
guide to AI SEO
.
The mechanics differ from classic ranking. An assistant breaks a question into smaller queries, a step AEO covers in its guide to
query fan out
. It then retrieves short passages from many pages and writes an answer from the passages that match best. A passage that opens with its main fact, uses the same name for the same thing every time, and makes sense without the paragraphs around it is easier to match than one that depends on its surroundings.
Accuracy is the other half of the work. An assistant may describe a company with an old product name, a competitor's framing or a plain guess. Checking how AI assistants describe a brand, and correcting the sources they draw from, is now a routine part of AI visibility work. Signals of experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trust still matter here, and AEO covers them in its guide to
E-E-A-T
.
AEO built the relaunched site around this practice. A spokesperson for the company described the shift in how people find information.
"Search used to reward the page that got the click. Now the answer gets read first, and the sources behind it either get named or they don't. We rebuilt aeo.so for people who want to be one of the named sources and would like to start without a budget conversation," said a spokesperson for AEO.
What does the relaunched aeo.so include?
The relaunched aeo.so includes two additions: new tactics for answer engine optimization and a set of free tools. Both are available on the same site, and visitors can read the tactics and use the tools without paying.
New tactics
The new tactics are aimed at the way AI assistants select sources. They concern how a page is written and structured so that its key facts can be lifted out cleanly and attributed to the right company. Readers who want background first can start with the guide on
AEO vs SEO
, which compares the two approaches.
Free tools
The free tools give a team a way to check its pages and its brand without a purchase. Because they cost nothing, a team can test an idea on one page before changing an entire site.
Teams that start this work usually follow a sequence. They list the questions their customers ask, check which pages answer those questions, rewrite the weak passages, and then check again whether assistants mention them. A free tool is most useful at the first and last steps, where a team needs a quick read on its current position and has no reason to commit a budget yet.
Who is the relaunch for?
The relaunched aeo.so is for marketers, content writers, site owners and agencies who are responsible for how a brand appears in AI-generated answers. It also suits small businesses that have no dedicated search team and need a place to begin.
These readers share one problem. Their content is judged by software that reads a question and a passage side by side, and the old signals, such as a high ranking or a large number of links, do not guarantee a mention. They need to know which of their pages get cited, which do not, and what to change.
The company expects most visitors to arrive with existing content rather than a blank page, so the tactics and tools start from what a site already publishes.
"Most teams already write good pages. What they lack is a way to see those pages the way an AI assistant does, one passage at a time. Free tools lower the cost of finding that out, and that is where most people should begin," said a spokesperson for AEO.
When and where is it available?
The relaunched site has been available at aeo.so since November 23, 2026. Anyone can open it in a web browser to read the new tactics and use the free tools. No installation is involved, because everything is hosted on the website.
AEO plans to keep adding material to the site as AI assistants and search engines change how they select and display sources. Practices that work today may shift as those systems are updated, which is the reason the company publishes its tactics as an ongoing body of work rather than a fixed guide.
