CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Cheyenne’s favorite 133-foot-long, 1.2-million-pound steam-powered locomotive is on a mission this holiday season: raise money for kids in need. The world-famous Big Boy will depart from Cheyenne this weekend, taking passengers whose tickets will directly support the Make-a-Wish Foundation.
Union Pacific’s
Big Boy No. 4014
just got off a nationwide tour this summer celebrating the United States of America’s 250th birthday, traveling from California to New York and spending July 4 in Philadelphia. Now, the engine is hitting the tracks on a shorter trip for a great cause.
Since mid-October, Union Pacific has been selling tickets to ride in vintage passenger cars the train will pull from Cheyenne to Denver. Proceeds from ticket sales will support the Make-A-Wish Foundation of America and include a donation from
Union Pacific’s Community Ties Giving Program
.
When families arrive in Denver, they’ll also get to meet Santa Claus, Mrs. Claus and some of their elf helpers, who will be handing out limited-edition gifts to children. See the
Union Pacific Website here
for more information.
