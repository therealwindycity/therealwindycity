Thinking About Visiting a Family Planning Clinic? Here’s What to Expect.
When you hear the words “family planning,” you might think these clinics are only for people looking for birth control or planning to start a family. But family planning clinics offer much more than that! From preventive health screenings and STI testing to reproductive health education and wellness services, Title X clinics provide care that supports overall health at different stages of life! If you’ve never visited a family planning clinic, you may have questions about what services are available, how much an appointment costs, or what to expect during your visit.
Title X (pronounced “Title Ten”) Family Planning clinics provide confidential, affordable family planning and preventive health services to individuals and families across Wyoming. Whether you’re scheduling your first appointment or simply looking for a place to access preventive care, here’s what you should know.
What Services Are Available?
Title X clinics offer more than birth control. Depending on the clinic, services may include:
Birth control counseling and contraceptive options
Pregnancy testing and counseling about pregnancy options
Sexually transmitted infection (STI) testing and treatment
Cervical and breast cancer screenings
Reproductive and sexual health education
Preconception counseling and preventive health services
Your healthcare provider can help you understand your options and determine which services are appropriate for your needs.
Do I Need Health Insurance?
No. You do not need health insurance to receive services at a Title X clinic. Services are provided on a sliding fee scale based on income and household size, and no one is turned away because of an inability to pay. Some services may be available at little or no cost, depending on your circumstances. If you have health insurance, you may also choose to use it for covered services. Having insurance does not automatically exclude you from the sliding fee scale.
Is My Appointment Confidential?
Yes. Confidentiality is an important part of Title X services. Services are available to adolescents and adults, and minors can receive Title X services without parental or guardian consent or notification. Patients are encouraged to involve a trusted adult when appropriate, but parental involvement is not required.
What Happens During an Appointment?
Your appointment will depend on the services you need. You may begin by discussing your health history, questions, and concerns with a healthcare provider. Depending on your visit, you may receive counseling, testing, screenings, or information about available healthcare options.
You are encouraged to ask questions, discuss your concerns, and take an active role in decisions about your healthcare. Not every appointment requires an examination or screening.
How Do I Find a Title X Clinic in Wyoming?
The Wyoming Health Council (WHC) is Wyoming’s Title X Family Planning grantee, supporting a network of clinics throughout the state. Title X clinics help make preventive and reproductive healthcare more accessible, including for individuals who are uninsured, underinsured, or have limited financial resources.
You don’t have to wait until you have a health concern to schedule an appointment. Preventive care is an important part of staying healthy.
