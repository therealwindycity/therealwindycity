Laramie County sheriff releases body cam footage of fatal officer-involved shooting

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The Laramie County Sheriff’s Office has released body camera footage captured by deputies during a fatal encounter with a suspected truck thief. The footage, the LCSO said, depicts the suspect taking a shooting stance before he is shot by officers, although it was later determined that he did not have a weapon.
The sheriff’s office announced Nov. 11 that a suspect was dead after an officer-involved shooting on the side of Interstate 80 east of Cheyenne.
At the time, details were sparse.
The office reported that deputies were responding to an incident, shots were fired and the suspect was pronounced dead at the scene. There was no additional threat to the public at that time.
The next day, the office announced that it was turning the case over to the Wyoming Division of Criminal Investigation to examine.
“Separately, LCSO will conduct an internal administrative review to evaluate the incident in relation to office policies, procedures and training standards to identify any areas needing improvement,” the LCSO said. “We understand the community has questions and we ask for your patience as both processes move forward. We will share additional information as it becomes available for public release.”
Nearly two weeks later, the office has released footage captured by the body cameras of the two deputies involved in the incident and their patrol vehicle. That video is below. Viewer discretion is advised.
Before the footage of the video plays, LCSO Public Information Officer Brandon Warner explains how body cameras work, noting that there is a 30-second video buffer that the cameras capture before they are activated in cases where deputies are forced to act quickly and forget to turn them on.
He later explains what led up to the fatal confrontation. According to him, deputies spotted a white flatbed truck that was reported stolen and attempted to pull it over. The truck, as the video depicts, did pull over at first, but sped off after deputies began to exit their patrol vehicle. They jumped back in the car and gave chase.
Deputies followed the truck along a stretch of Interstate 80 near the TA Travel Center and southwest of Hillsdale for some time. The video shows the truck cut across the grassy median on the interstate and quickly change direction multiple times, which the LCSO said put public lives at risk.
After driving through a fence, the suspect stopped the truck and began running across the interstate. Officers followed in their vehicle for a short distance until the suspect tripped and, according to the sheriff’s office, appeared to reach into his pocket as if for a weapon.
He continued away from the road until deputies caught up with him on foot, at which point he is seen on video turning around. Deputies said he assumed a “shooting stance,” facing deputies as if he was going to fire a weapon. Deputies engaged, shooting the suspect multiple times as he fell to the ground.
Not captured on video was the deputies reportedly attempting to render aid to the suspect. He was pronounced dead on the scene and was found to not have a gun.
Wyoming DCI will continue its investigation before turning over findings for Laramie County District Court review. The sheriff’s office, meanwhile, will continue an internal administrative review to evaluate the incident in relation to office policies, procedures and training standards.
