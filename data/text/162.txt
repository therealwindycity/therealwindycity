Mild Thanksgiving likely before possible weekend snow
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The National Weather Service in Cheyenne is predicting a clear and mild Thanksgiving for the capital city, though winter weather is expected to arrive with the weekend.
Today, the NWS in Cheyenne is predicting a high of 57 degrees and sunny skies. Wind speeds are also expected to remain fairly calm throughout the day, with wind speeds ranging from 5 to 15 mph.
At night, temps are likely to drop to a low of 39 as wind speeds pick up, climbing to 30 mph after midnight.
Friday is expected to be cloudier and far windier; the NWS in Cheyenne forecast calls for 50 mph wind gusts throughout the day.
That night, a slight chance of rain showers is expected after 11 p.m., possibly mixing with snow in the early Saturday morning hours as temperatures drop to a low around freezing.
On Saturday, the chance of snow and wintry mix climbs to 70%, expected to become all snow after 2 p.m. Temperatures are likely to reach a high of 45 degrees later in the day, though wind gusts as strong as 55 mph will make conditions feel cooler.
