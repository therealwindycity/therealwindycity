Cheyenne to see slightly cooler day before warmer Thanksgiving

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Cheyenne will see another slightly chilly day today before temps rise again for a mild Thanksgiving, Black Friday and holiday weekend. Some rain and snow could fall in the area Saturday.
According to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne, Wednesday in the capital city will be slightly cooler, with temps rising to a daytime high of 46 degrees. Mild winds, averaging 5–10 mph, are also expected. Later this evening, temps will dip to as low as 24 degrees.
Thanksgiving, meanwhile, is shaping up to be much more pleasant. Cheyenne residents can expect a high of 56 degrees and winds may blow a slightly faster 5–15 mph.
Temps will stay barely above freezing Thursday night, dropping to a low of 34 degrees. Winds will pick up to around 10–20 mph, increasing to 20–30 mph after midnight. The occasional gust could blow as fast as 45 mph.
Black Friday will be very breezy, but should otherwise be pleasant. Above-average temps will continue through the rest of the weekend, disrupted only by potential rain and snow Saturday and Saturday night.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
