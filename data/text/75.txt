Select South Triad schools in lockdown due to police activity in area
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A handful of South Triad schools are currently in lockdown due to a large police presence in south Cheyenne, Cheyenne police announced on Facebook Thursday morning. There is no active threat to the community at this time.
Goins Elementary School, South High School and Johnson Junior High School,
which all make up a larger school neighborhood in southwest Cheyenne
, are under lockdown. When they will leave lockdown was not shared by police.
The large police presence is not due to an active threat at the school, but rather as a precaution as police work nearby. See the
Cheyenne Police Department Facebook page
for more information.
Cap City News will also share information as it becomes available.
