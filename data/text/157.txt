Wyoming man sentenced to 15 years in federal prison for sexual abuse of minors

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A Wyoming man has been sentenced to 15 years in federal prison for the sexual abuse of multiple minors.
A release from the U.S. Department of Justice says that 52-year-old Reland Paul Littleshield Sr., of Arapahoe, Wyoming, has been sentenced to 15 years’ imprisonment and another 15 years of supervised release for sexual abuse and attempted aggravated sexual abuse.
The release says that Chief U.S. District Court Judge Kelly H. Rankin imposed the sentence.
“According to court documents, the offenses involved separate, predatory incidents of
sexual assault and attempted aggravated sexual abuse targeting two minors on the Wind
River Indian Reservation,” the release states. “In both cases, Littleshield deliberately supplied the minors with alcohol until they were severely intoxicated or unconscious before transporting them to his residence in Arapahoe. Once at the home, he physically overpowered, restrained, and sexually assaulted the incapacitated victims while they were unable to consent or escape.”
Littleshield pleaded guilty to two federal Class A felony counts: one count of sexual abuse and one count of attempted aggravated sexual abuse.
“Preying on children is a depraved and cowardly act that inflicts lifelong trauma” said U.S. Attorney Darin Smith. “Littleshield used alcohol as a lure to incapacitate and exploit minors. This 15-year sentence ensures he’ll be removed from society for a decade and a half.”
The Bureau of Indian Affairs investigated the case. Assistant U.S. Attorney Kerry J. Jacobson prosecuted the crime.
