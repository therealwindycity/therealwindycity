Cheyenne man, claiming man he reportedly attacked was sex offender, charged with aggravated assault
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A man was arrested in Cheyenne last weekend on aggravated assault and battery charges after he reportedly beat a man he claimed was a pedophile in the Cheyenne Depot Plaza late at night.
Sebastian Basham, born in 1998, was arrested in the very early hours of Nov. 15 for aggravated assault and battery causing serious bodily injury, a felony with a maximum potential penalty of 10 years in prison. He is considered innocent until pleading or being found guilty.
Cheyenne Police Department officers said in Basham’s criminal affidavit that they were dispatched to the Cheyenne Depot Plaza after midnight on Sunday, Nov. 15 to calls of an assault. A man whom officers were not able to get ahold of later reportedly told police that one man beat up another before they both left.
Officers said they were first able to catch up to the man that was reportedly the victim of the assault. He was bleeding profusely from his mouth and nose and had a swollen face. He told police that the other man, Basham, assaulted him, the affidavit states. He said that Basham knew who he was, called him a pedophile multiple times and hit him in the face.
He reportedly struggled giving additional details; he said he thought he had a concussion and was having a hard time remembering what had happened just moments before.
Police found Basham blocks away, at the intersection of Reed Avenue and 18th Street. He reportedly told police that the other man was a registered sex offender and was being rude to him. He added that he wanted the other man to know he was “less than dirt,” the affidavit states.
After he was placed in a patrol car, Basham also commented on a picture of the other man on the car’s in-computer screen and reportedly made threatening statements against him.
The other man was told he should go to the hospital after being assessed by Cheyenne Fire and Rescue, but refused.
Basham, meanwhile, was arrested and is being held on bond for $10,000. He has a preliminary hearing scheduled for Nov. 23.
