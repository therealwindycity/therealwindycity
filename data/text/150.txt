Former Cheyenne Regional Airport terminal (Arts Cheyenne Charette Report)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board will hold a special meeting on Wednesday, Dec. 2 at 6 p.m. to discuss the historic 1960s-era terminal at the Cheyenne Regional Airport and its future.
According to the City of Cheyenne, the meeting will take place in the
Municipal Building’s City Council Chambers
. The meeting can also be attended virtually
via Zoom
.
For more information, see the
Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board web page
.
