$10K bond upheld for man charged with late-night Depot Plaza assault

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A man’s $10,000 cash-only bond, issued after he reportedly assaulted another man in downtown Cheyenne Nov. 15 for being on the Sex Offender Registry, remains intact as his case heads to Laramie County District Court.
Sebastian Basham, born in 1998, was arrested in the very early hours of Nov. 15 for aggravated assault and battery causing serious bodily injury, a felony with a maximum potential penalty of 10 years in prison. He is considered innocent until pleading or being found guilty.
He appeared for his preliminary hearing in Laramie County Circuit Court on Nov. 23. The honorable judge Antoinette Williams presided, ultimately finding probable cause to bind his case to district court.
Cheyenne Police Department officers said in Basham’s criminal affidavit that they were dispatched to the Cheyenne Depot Plaza after midnight on Sunday, Nov. 15 to calls of an assault. A woman whom officers were not able to get ahold of later reportedly told police that one man beat up another before they both left.
All parties dispersed from the plaza before officers could arrive, a Cheyenne police officer said before the court, but they did manage to find the reported victim of the assault a few blocks away.
“He was bleeding profusely from his nose and face,” the officer said in court. “There was a chunk of something on his face — didn’t know if it was boogers or flesh.”
The assaulted man reportedly told the officer that the reason the altercation happened was because he was listed on the Sex Offender Registry, which Basham reportedly kept up with.
The man couldn’t answer many other questions at the time, however, as he was reportedly hurt badly and displayed symptoms consistent with a concussion.
Officers located Basham a few blocks away as he headed on foot toward MLK Park west of downtown. When officers pulled up to Basham, the officer said in court, Basham said “you got me” before officers asked him a single question. He was reportedly very proud of the incident, the officer added.
Basham was arrested and charged with aggravated assault and battery. He also reportedly earned a behavior complaint at the Laramie County Detention Center when he told a booking deputy that he would be training during his time in jail so that he could rack up an attempted murder charge against the same man when he gets out.
Given that, Williams moved to continue Basham’s $10,000 cash bond. He will soon appear in Laramie County District Court.
