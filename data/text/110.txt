Holiday Season Shortcuts to Entertain With Less Stress

(StatePoint) If you spend more time in the kitchen while holiday hosting than with your guests, the experience may feel more stressful than satisfying. Rather than missing out on all the moments that make the season special, let the frozen food aisle be your holiday season hack this year. In addition to quick breakfasts, snacks and weeknight meals, frozen foods can also be the starting point for creative, crowd-pleasing holiday dishes.
Frozen selections offer a convenient foundation for festive appetizers, breakfast buffets and shareable party spreads according to Ruiz Foods, the largest frozen Mexican food manufacturer in the United States. By adding a few of your favorite ingredients and colorful finishing touches to make it your own, you can serve authentic Mexican-inspired flavors without the day-long prep.
These time-saving strategies from Ruiz Foods incorporate both classic
El Monterey
favorites such as burritos, chimichangas and taquitos, as well as Más Bold — the brand’s newest taquito product line:
• Plan a Mexican fiesta from your freezer using burritos, chimichangas and enchiladas, prepared according to package directions, and set out bowls of shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, cheese, salsa, guacamole and sour cream. Add Mexican rice, black beans or tortilla chips to round out the spread. Guests can make their own selections and add toppings to suit their tastes. You will spend less time assembling individual plates and more time enjoying the party.
• Create a festive taquito board by arranging crispy
El Monterey Mas Bold Taquitos
or your preferred filling, best prepared in the air fryer, on a large platter or serving board. Surround them with small bowls of guacamole, salsa, queso, and sour cream. Fill in the spaces with cherry tomatoes, sliced jalapeños, colorful bell peppers, cilantro and lime wedges. The red and green ingredients add a festive holiday touch, while the variety of dips and dressings such as ranch, blue cheese or spicy mayonnaise lets guests create their own combinations.
• Turn prepared taquitos into
Mexican-inspired loaded nachos
by arranging them on a platter and adding refried beans, shredded cheese, sour cream, ranch or blue cheese dressing, guacamole, pico de gallo, lettuce and sliced jalapeños.
• Holiday entertaining doesn’t have to mean buying a long list of items for multiple recipes. Shop smarter by building your menu around versatile ingredients. Start in the frozen food aisle and choose two or three Mexican-inspired favorites that can serve as the foundation for your menu. Then select toppings and sides that work with everything. Rice, beans, salsa, shredded cheese, sour cream, ranch or blue cheese dressing, spicy mayonnaise, tomatoes and guacamole can complement taquitos, burritos and enchiladas. Using the same ingredients in different ways simplifies shopping, reduces preparation time and helps you make the most of your grocery budget.
• Keep a few frozen favorites stocked for last-minute get-togethers or quick family breakfasts, snacks or meals, eliminating the need for an extra shopping trip.
• Prepare toppings and dips in advance, set out serving dishes the night before, and choose foods that don’t require complicated last-minute preparation.
Based on Mexican-inspired favorites, El Monterey Más Bold offers four unique and authentic flavors: Chicken Bacon Ranch, Spicy Chipotle Beef, Buffalo-Style Chicken and Philly-Style Cheesesteak. For recipes and information about where to purchase El Monterey visit
https://elmonterey.com/where-to-buy/
.
The holidays are one of the busiest entertaining times of the year. Fortunately, simple, crowd-pleasing solutions that go from freezer to table in minutes can be easy to prepare and fun to eat, giving you one less thing to worry about.
