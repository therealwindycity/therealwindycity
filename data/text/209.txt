1 dead, 1 in critical condition after crash outside of Cheyenne
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — One person and a dog are dead and another person is in critical condition following a crash outside of Cheyenne on Wednesday.
A social media post from the Wyoming Highway Patrol says that shortly after 1 p.m. Wednesday, troopers were dispatched to a head-on collision on U.S. Highway 85, near milepost 3.7, south of Cheyenne.
The post says that troopers arrived on the scene to find two vehicles involved. According to a preliminary investigation by the WHP, a Subaru was traveling southbound on U.S. 85 behind an armored car when it attempted to pass. It collided head on with a northbound Toyota Tacoma.
The occupants of both vehicles required extrication from fire rescue personnel.
The driver and passenger of the Toyota were extricated from the vehicle, and both were airlifted to a trauma center in Colorado. The driver is listed in critical condition and the passenger is listed in stable condition.
The driver of the Subaru, a 65-year-old male from Scotts Bluff County, Nebraska, was also extricated, but was pronounced deceased at the scene.
The report also says that a dog in the Subaru was pronounced deceased.
The preliminary investigation states that speed and driver inattention are possible contributing factors in the crash.
The investigation is ongoing, and the Wyoming Highway Patrol is asking for anyone that may have witnessed this crash, or may have video, to contact the WHP dispatch at
307-777-4321
and reference incident number 2026-00091358.
“The Wyoming Highway Patrol would like to offer our deepest condolences to the family and friends of the driver that died in this crash,” the post states. “We also wish a speedy recovery to those that were hospitalized in this crash.”
This story contains preliminary information as provided by the Wyoming Highway Patrol via the Wyoming Department of Transportation Fatal Crash Summary map. The agency advises that information may be subject to change.
