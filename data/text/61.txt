Cheyenne’s first snow record falls today as forecast calls for warm, windy Friday
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Today is the day: Cheyenne is set to break a 126-year record for the latest measurable snowfall of the season despite cooler temperatures moving into the area.
The National Weather Service in Cheyenne has not recorded measurable snow this fall. According to records dating back to 1883, the last time Cheyenne made it to Nov. 20 without snow was during the 1900–1901 season.
With no snowfall expected in Cheyenne today, the city will break the record for the latest first measurable snowfall of the season. The old record for latest first snowfall was set on Nov. 20, 1900. (National Weather Service graphic)
Cheyenne’s next chance for a tenth of an inch or more of snow is around 50% on Tuesday, Nov. 24, with slim chances through the rest of the month, according to the forecast.
In the meantime, a windy day is forecast for Laramie County. West-southwest winds will increase from 5–15 mph this morning to 20–30 mph this afternoon. Gusts could reach up to 50 mph. The NWS in Cheyenne has issued a high wind warning for the southern Laramie Range and foothills from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
Expect widespread gusts of 40–55 mph across the area today, with localized gusts of up to 65 mph possible, mainly in wind-prone areas. This will also lead to elevated fire danger today over the High Plains. (National Weather Service graphic)
Today’s high will be near 52 degrees under sunny skies. Winds should ease to 10–15 mph after midnight, with gusts of up to 35 mph as the low drops to around 22.
Saturday will be sunny with a high near 42, accompanied by west winds at 10–15 mph gusting at up to 25 mph. Saturday night will be mostly clear with a low around 17.
Sunday will be mostly sunny with a high near 38 and an overnight low around 19.
Next week, Cheyenne will see partly sunny skies Monday with a high near 42 and a chance of snow showers before 11 p.m., followed by mostly sunny skies and a high near 43 on Tuesday. Temperatures will warm slightly ahead of Thanksgiving, with a high near 49 on Wednesday and 46 on Thanksgiving Day under mostly sunny skies.
