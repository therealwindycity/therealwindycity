Johnson Junior High’s Kyleigh Tjaden is LCSD1 student of the week for Nov. 30
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Kyleigh Tjaden, an eighth grader at Johnson Junior High, is Laramie County School District 1’s student of the week for Nov. 30. Her leadership and academic achievements earned her the recognition.
Tjaden is a straight-A student. She’s maintained good grades throughout her eighth-grade year at Johnson and even serves on the student council, where she helps make valuable decisions on behalf of the student body.
She is a hard worker after school, too, the district said. She plays on both the volleyball and girls wrestling and will appear in Johnson’s upcoming winter play. In her free time, she bakes.
Tjaden selected Annika Nelson as her favorite teacher, LCSD1 said, because Nelson understands and supports her.
