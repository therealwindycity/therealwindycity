Cheyenne nears breaking 126-year record for latest first snow

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Cheyenne is on track to break a 126-year-old record for its latest first measurable snowfall as dry, mild weather holds over the capital city through Friday.
With no snow in the forecast for today or Friday, Cheyenne is set to pass the existing record set during the 1900–01 season, when measurable snow of at least a tenth of an inch did not fall until Nov. 20, according to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne.
With records dating back to 1883, these are the top 10 latest snowfalls in Cheyenne. This year will break the record, which has stood for more than a century. (National Weather Service graphic)
While snow fell Oct. 15 and accumulated on the north and west outskirts of town, it did not stick to the snow board at the weather service’s observing station in Cheyenne, meteorologists said.
“It is not impossible, nor even particularly unlikely, that this snow-less streak will last into December,” meteorologists wrote in the forecast discussion.
Today will be partly sunny with a high near 51 degrees. A breeze from the west will shift to come from the south-southeast this morning. Tonight will be partly cloudy with a low around 29.
Gusty winds return Friday, which is expected to feature sunny skies and a high near 49. West-southwest winds reaching 15 mph will ramp up to 20–30 mph in the afternoon, with gusts reaching 40 mph. Friday night will be partly cloudy with a low near 23 and gusts of up to 35 mph.
Cheyenne’s next shot at snow comes early next week. Meteorologists said there’s about a 50% chance for at least a tenth of an inch of snow Monday night into Tuesday, followed by a 20%–30% chance around Thanksgiving.
Colder air settles into Laramie County this weekend, bringing temps that feel more like November. Saturday will be sunny with a high near 42 and west-northwest gusts of up to 25 mph. Saturday night will be mostly clear with a low around 18.
Sunday will be mostly sunny with a high near 42 and an overnight low near 21. Monday’s high will reach 41, with snow chances increasing Monday night into Tuesday, when the high will reach 39.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
