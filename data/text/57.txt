East High junior James Shafer-Barrera is LCSD1 student of the week for Nov. 23
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — East High School junior James Shafer-Barrera is Laramie County School District 1’s student of the week for Nov. 23. He was selected for his steadfast service, both in his school and in the community.
Shafer-Barrera is known for his longtime commitment to Key Club, which he has participated in for three years. He has participated in nearly every community and school service project that he could in that time.
He is often the first to arrive and the last to leave, LCSD1 said. His volunteer work includes East High beautification projects, greenway cleanup, dishwashing at the Frontier Days Pancake Breakfast and volleyball concessions.
During Frontier Days, he also volunteers as a showtime operator, often putting in six- to 12-hour shifts to make sure the event runs smoothly and is a good time for the rest of the community. He’s always thinking about how he next wants to contribute to the community, the district said; he said he is planning on volunteering at the Cheyenne Regional Medical Center over winter break.
Shafer-Barrera is on top of his classwork, too. He is good about his attendance and grades, taking courses he thinks aligns with his goal to become a certified emergency medical responder. He said he may also join the Air National Guard to help fund further education.
He selected Tyler Kupec as his favorite teacher because Kupec takes the time to explain concepts and guide students through questions when they need help.
