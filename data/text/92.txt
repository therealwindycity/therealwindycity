See downtown Cheyenne buzzing with holiday spirit Saturday
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Downtown Cheyenne came alive with holiday lights and spirit Saturday. Starting with the inaugural Holiday Night Market and ending with the official Depot Plaza lighting ceremony, the cozy winter feelings downtown were infectious.
Cheyenne residents participated in three main events downtown Nov. 21
. The Holiday Night Market took over 17th Street, with vendors selling all sorts of wares under the warm string lights decorating the road.
Nearby, crowds gathered in the Depot Plaza in anticipation for the lighting of the Christmas Tree and the Depot itself. Before Cheyenne could count down and turn the lights on, the Lighting the Legacy mounted wreath procession had to wrap up its journey downtown from the Wyoming Capitol. On the way, participants honored Capitol Avenue’s many bronze statues with evergreen wreaths.
