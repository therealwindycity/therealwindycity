Dumfries, Virginia - November 23, 2026 -
Patty Blackwelder of Samson Properties listed 17152 Gullwing Dr, a renovated four-bedroom house in the Potomac Shores community of Dumfries, on Nov. 14 at $990,000. Zillow now shows the listing accepting backup offers. In the same week, her five-bedroom listing at 18001 Marsh Pine Rd in the community closed on Nov. 16 for $927,000.
The house is a Coastal Craftsman James model built by Ryan Homes in 2016 and renovated in 2022, with 3,915 square feet over three finished levels. The main floor has an eat-in kitchen with quartz counters, double ovens and an island, a family room with a gas fireplace, a formal dining room and a study with built-ins. The walk-out basement includes a recreation room, and outside are a screened porch, a stamped patio with a smokeless fire pit and a fenced backyard with an irrigation system.
"This home is everything today's buyer is looking for: turnkey condition and a lifestyle unmatched in the area," Blackwelder said.
Blackwelder has closed 174 home sales worth about $133.3 million over the past five years, according to
Homes.com
, and 123 of them were in Potomac Shores.
She has two more listings in Potomac Shores. 2020 Silver Sycamore Ln, listed at $790,000, is a 2022 Pulte Craftsman on a cul-de-sac backing to woods. 17506 Spring Cress Dr, listed at $775,000, has 3,096 square feet, fully paid-off solar panels and raised garden beds, and the seller is offering $10,000 toward the buyer's closing costs.
Potomac Shores residents share pools, a fitness center, trails, the Jack Nicklaus Signature Golf Club and a community pier on the Potomac River. A future VRE commuter rail station and a town center are planned for the community.
