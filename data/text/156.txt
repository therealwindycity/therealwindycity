Serving suspension for ‘impermissible communication’ with transfers, UW’s Sawvel to miss out on Saturday game
LARAMIE, Wyo. — The University of Wyoming Cowboys will play their final game of the season against the University of Connecticut Huskies on Saturday, but they’ll have to do it without their head coach. Jay Sawvel, currently in his third season coaching the Pokes, will serve a one-game suspension this weekend after engaging in impermissible conduct with transfers.
The suspension, the University of Wyoming Athletics Department said in a statement Tuesday, is self-imposed. The coach and university state that Sawvel violated NCAA rules last December when he engaged in talks with student-athletes who had announced their intent to transfer to Wyoming via social media, but had not yet made the move official.
The talks were a violation of
NCAA Bylaw 13.1.1.3
, which states that a university’s “athletics staff member or other representative of the institution’s athletics interests shall not communicate or make contact with the student-athlete of another NCAA Division I institution, or any individual associated with the student-athlete (e.g., family member, scholastic or nonscholastic coach, advisor), directly or indirectly, without first obtaining authorization through the notification of transfer process.”
UW wishes to remain compliant with those bylaws, it continued in its statement, leaving Sawvel out for the Saturday matchup.
Wyoming, which ended its conference slate with a 6–2 record, is still fighting for a spot in the Mountain West Conference Championship. The team, which lost earlier this year to contenders in North Dakota State and New Mexico, will be counting on tiebreakers from other conference matchups happening this weekend. This weekend’s game against UConn, which will now be coached by defensive ends coach Brian Hendricks, will have no bearing on whether or not Wyoming makes the championship.
