DAVENPORT, Iowa - November 24, 2026 - PRESSADVANTAGE -
Northwest Plumbing, Heating & AC
is drawing attention to the importance of furnace repair and winter plumbing preparation as temperatures begin to fall across the Quad Cities area. The locally owned company, which has served the region since 1923, is encouraging area homeowners to address heating and plumbing concerns before the coldest weeks of the season arrive.
The transition from cooling to heating is a period when many households discover problems with their furnaces. According to the company, furnaces can begin drawing more power than necessary as they age, as dust accumulates on filters, and as electronic components wear down. These factors can lead to inadequate heating and higher energy costs. Common issues that may require professional attention include a failing heat exchanger, a fraying blower belt, worn bearings in the motor, and loose panels in the ductwork.
Northwest Plumbing, Heating & AC provides furnace repair, installation, and annual heating system checks, along with service for boilers, mini-splits, geothermal systems, steam heat, and high-efficiency equipment. The company recommends a central air inspection before peak demand arrives, allowing technicians to identify worn components and efficiency concerns before a system fails during the coldest stretch of winter.
"Preparing a heating system before the cold settles in is one of the most practical steps a homeowner can take," said Bill Durand, a representative of Northwest Plumbing, Heating & AC. "Many furnace failures that occur in January can be traced to small issues that were present in the fall. An early inspection gives technicians the chance to correct those problems before they turn into emergencies."
Plumbing concerns also increase during the winter months, making year-round plumbing a natural companion to seasonal heating work. The company addresses frozen pipes, sump pump failures, and water heater problems, in addition to drain cleaning, sewer repair, camera inspection, and leak repair.
"Cold weather places added strain on both heating and plumbing systems," Durand said. "Frozen pipes and sump pump failures tend to appear at the least convenient times, so attention to these systems in advance helps households avoid disruption during winter."
Northwest Plumbing, Heating & AC also offers preventative maintenance programs and financing options for larger projects such as furnace replacements. The company maintains a seasonal Deal of the Month, with current details available on their website. Homeowners interested in seasonal updates can follow the company on
Facebook
, where recent posts have included practical maintenance tips for area residents.
Having served the Quad Cities community for more than a century, the company reports a familiarity with the demands of local winters. Additional background on its seasonal recommendations has been shared in a recent
news article
describing the company's approach to furnace readiness.
