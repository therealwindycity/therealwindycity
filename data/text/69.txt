Threads of Hope
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Threads of Hope, a local nonprofit that supports kids in foster care with clothing, is getting in on the holiday fun this year with the Reindeer Run on Dec. 5. The event sends participants around town collecting stamps for a prize and a meeting with Father Christmas himself.
According to Threads of Hope, runners’ journeys will start at the Cheyenne Depot Plaza. Inside, near the coffee shop, kids will be given a card and directed to following the Reindeer Run route, where they will make five stops to collect stamps.
The run will end at the
Threads of Hope headquarters
, where Santa will be waiting to meet kids and give them a gift.
The event will also mark the grand opening of the nonprofit’s Foster Closet. The closet will help better support the organization’s goal of making sure Cheyenne’s foster kids are clothed.
“Come celebrate this exciting new chapter with us as we continue spreading hope, love and kindness to children and families throughout Laramie County,”
Threads of Hope said in a Facebook post announcing the event
. “This isn’t just a Christmas adventure. It’s a community coming together to remind children that they are seen, valued, loved and supported.”
The event begins at 2:30 p.m. Dec. 5. See the
Threads of Hope Facebook page
for more information.
