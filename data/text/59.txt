LCCC gets TIME Magazine nod as one of America’s best community colleges

Laramie County Community College (Photo by Stephanie Lam / Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Laramie County Community College is up there with the best of the best, according to TIME Magazine. The college announced Friday that the magazine placed the school on its list of the top community colleges in the nation.
LCCC is just one of two Wyoming community colleges on the list, LCCC said. It was chosen for its student success, educational quality and workforce impact. Access and affordability are also heavily considered.
The only other Wyoming school on the list is Northern Wyoming Community College District, serving Sheridan and Johnson counties.
“This distinction reflects the outstanding work of our faculty and staff and, most importantly, the success of our students,” Dr. Joe Schaffer, LCCC president, said in a school release. “Every day, we see lives transformed through education, training and opportunity.”
“Whether students are pursuing workforce credentials, associate degrees, or an applied bachelor’s degree, we remain committed to helping them achieve their goals and build stronger futures for themselves, their families, and our communities,” he added.
LCCC already serves thousands of students each year through academic transfer programs, career and technical education, workforce training, dual and concurrent enrollment opportunities and applied bachelor’s degree programs. The college partners with industry, community organizations and K–12 schools to create pathways that support workforce development and economic vitality throughout Wyoming.
The TIME nod comes as LCCC continues to expand educational opportunities. From short-term credentials and industry certifications to associate’s and bachelor’s degrees, the college said it remains focused on preparing graduates for continued education, career advancement and lifelong success.
“This recognition affirms our mission of transforming lives through the power of inspired learning,” Schaffer said. “We are proud to serve our students and communities and grateful for the trust they place in us each day.”
