LCSO arrests 69-year-old driver involved in fatal October car vs. bike crash
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The Laramie County Sheriff’s Office announced Wednesday that it has arrested 69-year-old Burns resident Wanda Richards for her reported involvement in an Oct. 4 collision that killed an 11-year-old bicyclist. She is formally charged with DUI-related aggravated vehicular homicide and reckless aggravated vehicular homicide.
On Sunday, Oct. 4, a young bicyclist was taken via helicopter to Colorado to seek medical treatment after a violent hit by a vehicle.
The boy was reportedly riding his bicycle around the intersection of County Roads 147 and 213, a little over a mile west of Burns, when he was hit. Traffic at that intersection is controlled by a one-way stop sign.
It was later confirmed that the child died to his injuries in the hospital.
Now the sheriff’s office is bringing charges to the woman it said was responsible for the incident. Richards was, according to a crash reconstruction, driving around the posted 55 mph speed limit when she approached the intersection where the boy and another young bicyclist were riding their bikes. She then “failed to reduce speed or move over while overtaking the children, leading to the collision,” the sheriff said.
A blood draw that Richards reportedly consented to at the scene came back positive for marijuana and methamphetamine.
She is now in the Laramie County Detention Center pending her initial appearance in Laramie County Circuit Court. She is presumed innocent until pleading or being found guilty.
