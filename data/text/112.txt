(StatePoint) Holiday shopping for a spa lover, wellness enthusiast or simply a busy person who needs some encouragement to slow down and unwind? Get inspired by these holiday gift ideas, all of which promote rest, relaxation and recuperation:
An Inflatable Hot Tub
Give the gift of year-round relaxation and turn your recipient’s backyard into an oasis so they can soak, unwind and reset, right at home. SaluSpa inflatable hot tubs set up in minutes and offer more affordable, portable alternatives to their traditional, hard-sided counterparts. SaluSpa inflatable hot tubs are easy to maintain, designed for comfort and durability, and feature freeze-resistant technology so they can provide spa-worthy comfort year-round.
One of the most popular models is the
SaluSpa Hot Springs
, available in a 2-4-person round size and 4-6-person round or square size, all of which deliver a relaxing bubble massage and heat up to 104 degrees F.
Two additional models, the SaluSpa
Hawaii
and the SaluSpa
Monterey
, both offer room for up to six people, making them great options for those who like to unwind with friends. Each model is a different style and color, suiting a variety of backyard designs.
The SaluSpa
Hollywood
, which also has room for up to six, offers two massage intensity levels, and color-changing LED lights in the semi-transparent liner for versatility and an overall warm, glowing ambiance.
Plus, if your recipient leads an active lifestyle, gifting a hot tub can support recovery from exercise, help release muscle tension, and promote overall relaxation of the body and mind. Wellness and relaxation await when you gift an inflatable hot tub this holiday season.
Plush Towels
Nothing beats the feeling of stepping out of the shower, pool or hot tub and enveloping yourself in a luxurious towel. Amp up these moments for your loved one by upgrading their linen closet. The best, most spa-like towels are plush, quick drying, anti-microbial, extra soft and absorbent, and made from durable, natural fibers.
To add some personalized flair, consider monogramming them with your gift recipient’s initials, name or favorite saying.
An At-Home Facial Kit
A gift certificate to a facialist is always appreciated, but with an at-home facial kit, you can help your gift recipient achieve the same glow without even having to leave their house. You’ll also be stretching your gifting dollar further as a kit can be used multiple times. And like in-spa facials, kits vary by skin goals, number of steps, and ingredients, making it easy to find the right one to suit your gift recipient’s needs and preferences. Pair the kit with some aromatherapy candles and they will never need to book a facial again.
This holiday season, support wellness and a stress-free mindset with gifts that give your recipient a reason to pause and reset at home.
