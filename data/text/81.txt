Cheyenne Depot Plaza, downtown lighting up with Christmas cheer Nov. 21
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Christmas is coming, Cheyenne. The community will make the holiday season official this week with a series of festive events downtown that kids and yuletide enthusiasts are not going to want to miss.
This holiday season, downtown Cheyenne is even starting new traditions, such as the Lighting the Legacy wreath-laying procession and the holiday night market. This brief guide may be used as a reference for all the festivities happening in downtown Cheyenne this Saturday.
Lighting the Legacy Wreath Procession
A mounted procession will travel across Cheyenne’s downtown Nov. 21
, decorating the many bronzes along Capitol Avenue with wreaths. Led my
the Mounted Marshals
, the Lighting the Legacy procession will honor the stories of those depicted by the statues, as well as the community effort that led to their creation.
The procession will depart from the Wyoming State Capitol at 4:30 p.m. It will travel south to the Depot Plaza, stopping as local leaders and business owners place a wreath at each bronze to honor their story.
Members of the Cheyenne community are welcome to watch and carry battery-operated LED candles to help create a corridor of light for the procession.
Depot Lighting Ceremony
By the time the procession is finished sprucing up Capitol Avenue’s bronzes, it’ll almost be time to light up the Depot Plaza with Christmas cheer. At 5:30 p.m., the Historic Cheyenne Depot will be lit up in Christmas colors as
Cheyenne Presents
and the
Downtown Development Authority
kick off the holiday season.
Afraid of attending alone? No worries: Santa Claus will be there. Families are invited to chat with Santa one last time before he hops into his sleigh and heads back to the North Pole to prepare for a busy holiday season.
This event is completely free to attend.
Holiday Night Market
Also near the Depot Plaza is the Holiday Night Market. The brand-new tradition will take place from 3:30 to 6:30 p.m. along 17th Street.
Sponsored by DowntownFirst and Community Action of Laramie County, the event brings a European-style holiday market feel to Cheyenne’s early holiday season celebrations.
“We have over 30 different artisans, food vendors and crafting vendors lined up from Central to Pioneer,” DowntownFirst Vice President Corey Loghry said in a release promoting the event.
Black Tooth Brewery will have a booth on the street, and other downtown bars and restaurants will offer their regular drink menus with some special holiday features for market patrons to enjoy while they shop and stroll the market, the release added. Several vendors will be offering beverages, including hot chocolate.
Some of the proceeds from the market will go to
Community Action of Laramie County, which provides housing and support to homeless veterans as well as housing, healthcare and self-sufficiency programs for low-income families and individuals
.
Can’t make it?
Cap City News
will be on the ground capturing photos and livestreaming the lighting ceremony.
