When Water Meets Electricity: How ELEGRP Advances Electrical Safety
Powered by its PBE™ Technology Platform, ELEGRP develops application-specific leakage protection solutions for electrical products operating in wet environments.
LAS VEGAS
,
Nov. 23, 2026
/PRNewswire/ -- From smart toilets and water flossers to water heaters, portable-pool equipment and pressure washers, more electrical products are designed for use near water, creating a growing challenge for manufacturers: ensuring electrical safety in wet environments.
While waterproofing and insulation remain essential, effective safety also requires detecting electrical faults and disconnecting power when necessary. ELEGRP addresses these challenges through its PBE™ Technology Platform, offering leakage protection solutions tailored to different applications.
Why Does Water Increase the Risk of Electric Shock?
Moisture reduces the electrical resistance of human skin, allowing more current to pass through the body under the same electrical conditions. According to the U.S. Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA), wet skin has significantly lower resistance than dry skin.
For products such as pressure washers, portable pools, smart toilets and water flossers,
water ingress protection and electrical leakage protection must work together
to reduce electrical risks.
Beyond Waterproofing: The Role of Active Leakage Protection
Traditional electrical safety relies heavily on waterproof structures and insulation. However, effective protection cannot depend solely on keeping water out.
Built on ELEGRP's
PBE™ Technology Platform
, its leakage protection devices integrate current detection and rapid-response protection. When residual current exceeds a preset threshold, the device rapidly disconnects power to help reduce the risk of electric shock.
The platform also features patented automatic self-test technology. Certain PBE-based devices perform self-tests every five seconds to help verify that their protection functions remain operational.
This approach shifts the focus from preventing water ingress alone to actively detecting electrical faults and responding quickly.
Why Does Leakage Protection Need to Be Application-Specific?
Different water-related products face different electrical risks and operating conditions. Protection must therefore be tailored to each application.
Through its PBE™ Technology Platform, ELEGRP develops solutions based on equipment characteristics, electrical parameters and operating environments. For outdoor equipment, such as pressure washers and portable pool systems, weather-resistant materials and UV-resistant designs help withstand sunlight, rain and temperature fluctuations. For water heaters, ELEGRP incorporates technology designed to detect abnormal voltage on the grounding conductor and provide corresponding protection.
The importance of ground-fault protection is also reflected in industry data. According to the Electrical Safety Foundation International (ESFI), U.S. electrocutions have declined by approximately 81% since GFCIs were introduced in homes, while electrocutions involving consumer products have declined by 93%.
As water-related applications expand, leakage protection is becoming a core consideration in product safety design.
