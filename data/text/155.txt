WyoMovies and Capitol Cinema are bringing back its best gift card deals of the year, and shoppers who get an early start can take advantage of free movie tickets, a free refillable popcorn bucket, and StudioCash back for Studio Rewards members.
The holiday gift card promotion begins Tuesday, November 24, and runs through Christmas Eve, with three different offers throughout the season. However, the biggest bonuses are available for just six days.
November 24–29: Free Movie Tickets and a Free 2027 Popcorn Bucket
If you’re planning to purchase WyoMovies gift cards this holiday season, November 24–29 is the time to visit Capitol Cinema.
For six days only, customers purchasing physical gift cards at their favorite WyoMovies theatre will receive:
One FREE holiday movie ticket for every $50 spent on WyoMovies gift cards, with a limit of four tickets per transaction.
A FREE 2027 WyoMovies Annual Refillable Popcorn Bucket with a gift card purchase of $100 or more.
10% back in StudioCash for Studio Rewards members.
The 2027 Annual Popcorn Bucket includes its first fill and can be filled with delicious movie theatre popcorn for just $5 now through 2027. The bucket bonus is available exclusively in theatres, with a limit of one free bucket per transaction.
Spend $100 on gift cards in theatres during this promotion, and you could walk away with two free movie tickets, a free popcorn bucket, and StudioCash rewards.
Prefer to shop online? Physical gift cards purchased at
GiveWyoMovies.com
during these six days also qualify for the free holiday ticket offer and 10% back in StudioCash.
Cyber Monday, November 30: One Day Only, Earn 20% Back in StudioCash
WyoMovies is offering its biggest StudioCash bonus of the holiday season with a special one-day online-only promotion on Monday, November 30 at
GiveWyoMovies.com
.
Purchase physical gift cards at
GiveWyoMovies.com
and receive:
One FREE holiday movie ticket for every $50 spent on gift cards, with a limit of four tickets per transaction.
20% back in StudioCash for Studio Rewards members.
Electronic gift cards purchased through
WyoMovies.com
will also qualify for 20% back in StudioCash, but are not eligible for the free holiday ticket bonus.
This special offer is available online for one day only.
A Gift They’ll Actually Use: Look What’s Coming to the Big Screen!
With some of Hollywood’s most anticipated movies arriving this December and throughout 2027, there’s no shortage of reasons to give someone a WyoMovies gift card this Christmas.
Moviegoers won’t have to wait long to put those gift cards to use. December’s lineup includes
Violent Night 2
,
Zero A.D.
,
Dune: Part Three
, and the highly anticipated
Avengers: Doomsday
. Tickets are already on sale for these upcoming releases.
And that’s just the beginning. Take a look at some of the major movies coming in 2027:
Narnia: The Magician’s Nephew
Sonic the Hedgehog 4
Godzilla x Kong: Supernova
The Legend of Zelda
Star Wars: Starfighter
How to Train Your Dragon 2
Spider-Man: Beyond the Spider-Verse
Shrek 5
A Minecraft Movie Squared
The Bluey Movie
Frozen 3
Avengers: Secret Wars
From family movie nights and date nights to some of the biggest blockbuster releases of the year, a WyoMovies gift card gives everyone something to look forward to. Plus, WyoMovies gift cards can be used at ANY WyoMovies theatre across the state!
And for anyone taking advantage of the free 2027 Annual Popcorn Bucket offer, there will be plenty of opportunities to enjoy those $5 refills throughout the year, with or without a movie. Stop by anytime for a $5 popcorn fill!
Don’t Miss the Best Gift Card Deals of the Year
Be sure to stop into Capitol Cinema before November 29 to take advantage of the free popcorn bucket and holiday movie ticket offers, or shop online November 30 to earn 20% back in StudioCash.

The Fine Print
Free holiday movie tickets are valid January 3 through February 17, 2027. Limit four free tickets per transaction. The free 2027 Annual Popcorn Bucket offer is available in theatres only from November 24–29, with a limit of one free bucket per transaction. StudioCash rewards are available to Studio Rewards members.
Physical gift cards ordered at GiveWyoMovies.com are shipped by U.S. Mail with Ground Advantage tracking. Delivery times vary depending on distance from Casper, Wyoming, and may take up to 10 days.
For last-minute holiday shopping, purchase a physical gift card at your local WyoMovies theatre or order an electronic gift card for delivery directly to your recipient’s email.
All movie titles, release dates, and availability are subject to change. Not all titles will play at all locations.
