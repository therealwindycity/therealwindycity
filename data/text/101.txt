Three Cheyenne residents, including juvenile, seriously injured in I-25 crash south of Colorado border Sunday

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A violent crash just south of the Wyoming–Colorado border Sunday night resulted in serious injuries for multiple Cheyenne residents, Colorado State Patrol confirmed Monday. The driver of one of the vehicles involved in the crash was arrested for careless driving causing serious bodily injury.
Traffic was obstructed on Interstate 25 during the evening of Sunday, Nov. 22 after a fiery crash. According to a report by the Colorado State Patrol, a northbound car struck another car that was pulled over partially within the lane of travel after it had hit a deer moments prior.
The parked car, a 2026 Buick Enclave, was driven by 29-year-old Hunter Herzberger. He was transporting a female adult and juvenile. They were pulled over after their collision with the deer.
They were not pulled over far enough to the side of the interstate, however, and were struck by a 2014 Ford Escape that was traveling north in the right lane. After being hit, the Buick was pushed forward while rotating counterclockwise. The Escape continued northbound while rotating clockwise, Colorado State Patrol said. Both vehicles caught fire during the crash and were completely engulfed in flames by the time first responders arrived.
Herzberger, the adult female Buick passenger and the two adult females in the Escape were taken by ground ambulance to a nearby hospital. The Buick passenger and the Escape driver were both seriously injured. The Buick’s juvenile female passenger, seriously injured and found unresponsive by first responders, was taken by helicopter to receive medical attention.
All five of the individuals involved are from Cheyenne. The current status of each injured person was not shared.
Wyoming Highway Patrol confirmed to Cap City News Monday afternoon that, originally believing the crash occurred north of the Wyoming-Colorado border, Wyoming troopers also responded to the scene. After learning the crash occurred in Colorado, the Wyoming Highway Patrol received permission to assist and it was a Wyoming trooper who ultimately rendered lifesaving aid to the juvenile.
Herzberger, who had reportedly not parked the Buick far enough off of the lane of travel, will be charged for careless driving causing serious bodily injury. He is considered innocent until pleading or being found guilty.
Cap City News will provide updates when they become available.
