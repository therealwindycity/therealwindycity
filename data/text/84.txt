Cheyenne native Tristan Johnson named Chamber of Commerce VP of operations
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The Greater Cheyenne Chamber of Commerce has named Tristan Johnson, a Cheyenne native with more than 20 years of experience in the nonprofit and private sectors, as its new vice president of operations. She started work Friday, Nov. 13.
This won’t be Johnson’s first time in the chamber; she previously served in a leadership role.
“Returning to the chamber feels like coming home,” Johnson said. “This organization and the Cheyenne community have always held a special place in my heart. I’m incredibly grateful for the opportunity to bring the experience I’ve gained over the past decade back to an organization I believe in so strongly. I look forward to supporting our staff, strengthening relationships with our members and helping the chamber continue to make a positive impact in our community.”
She also serves on the Executive Committee of the Cheyenne Animal Shelter Board of Directors, volunteers with Cheyenne Frontier Days through the WHEELS organization and continues to support the Chamber’s Leadership Cheyenne program.
Johnson is also working toward her Master of Business Administration.
