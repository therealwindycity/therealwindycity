GORDONSVILLE, Tenn. - Nov. 23, 2026 -
Komline broke ground Nov. 12 on a 20,000-square-foot expansion and renovation of its existing Gordonsville manufacturing facility. The Gordonsville, Tennessee facility is Komline-Filtra's primary manufacturing site, where it fabricates and assembles industrial filtration systems for the automotive, wastewater, and oil and gas markets. Its current product line includes coolant filtration for automotive clean machining, standard wastewater filtration systems, and rapid-deployment wastewater systems that deliver immediate regulatory compliance
.
The expanded facility will manufacture the Internalift® and Externalift® screw pump product lines, which
Komline acquired from Xylem
in September, enabling the company to serve municipal wastewater treatment customers in addition to its industrial clients.
Internalift® and Externalift® screw pumps move large volumes of wastewater and entrained solids at treatment plant headworks, between treatment stages and at the effluent end, handling influent, return sludge and stormwater pumping. With more than 2,000 installations worldwide, the product lines complement Komline’s screening, clarification, filtration, thickening, dewatering and sludge drying technologies.
Company leaders and employees were joined at the ceremony by Tanner Cox, a representative of U.S. Senator Bill Hagerty; Smith County Mayor Jeff Mason; Smith County Commissioner Richey Gregory; Bill Woodard of the Smith County Chamber of Commerce; Wanda Geho of Upper Cumberland Electric; and representatives of the Tennessee Department of Economic and Community Development. Construction is expected to be completed in March 2027.
The expansion reflects Komline’s commitment to Gordonsville operations and workforce. As production evolves, the company plans to hire area residents for a variety of positions, including production support staff, welders and machinists.
“Adding 20,000 square feet and modern manufacturing equipment, including laser cutting, plate rolling, press brakes and automated welding, gives our Gordonsville team the tools to build with greater precision and efficiency,” said James Sauk, vice president of operations for Komline’s Industrial Group. “It builds on what this facility already does well and positions us to serve our customers from Gordonsville for many years to come.”
To invest in the local workforce, Komline plans to partner with area trade schools and workforce programs, giving students and job seekers a path into careers in welding, machining and manufacturing.
For information about employment opportunities, visit
komline.com/join-our-team
.
About Komline
Founded in 1946, Komline designs and delivers mission-critical separation and water treatment technologies for municipalities and industrial operations. The company helps customers turn complex water and industrial challenges into reliable, efficient solutions — whether producing clean drinking water, treating wastewater for safe discharge, or recovering valuable materials in industrial and agricultural processes.
