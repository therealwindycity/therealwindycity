CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The City of Cheyenne’s watering schedule ends Nov. 30, the Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities reminded residents Thursday. Any lawn watering past that date and until March 31 is strictly prohibited.
With warmer temperatures lingering in the region, it may be tempting for homeowners and businesses to continue watering lawns, the BOPU said. However, watering anything but gardens and trees after Dec. 1 is not allowed.
“The watering schedule is in place to promote efficient water use while preparing residents for potential future water shortages due to a Colorado River curtailment,” the board said. “The BOPU will continue to evaluate water resources and make appropriate adjustments as needed.”
Even before the schedule ends, residents are not allowed to water during high winds, rain storms or snowstorms and are prohibited from allowing water to needlessly run off into the street.

Cheyenne BOPU website
for more information.
