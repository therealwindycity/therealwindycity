Former owner of Cheyenne restaurant Napoli’s planning new Mediterranean eatery
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A new business venture by the previous owner of local Italian restaurant Napoli’s is coming to Cheyenne. The restaurant, called Fig and Olive, will primarily feature Mediterranean cuisine and is replacing
Paris West
, which is soon to close.
Local businessperson Dardan Selimi appeared before the Cheyenne City Council on Monday night as the new restaurant looks to get established and secure a bar and grill liquor license. There, he had the opportunity to tell the council a little bit about the restaurant and what future diners should expect.
“We want to be a welcoming place where people can enjoy a nice dinner and also have some glasses of wine or beer or cocktails with their meals,” Selimi said.
He told the council about his previous experience at Napoli’s, one of Cheyenne’s premier downtown fine dining options. There, he said, he started in the kitchen before working his way up to ownership.
“I handled all the day-to-day operations,” he said. “That experience taught me how important it is to train the staff, make sure alcohol is being served responsibly.”
He emphasized that all staff will undergo the proper training and strict ID verification to ensure responsible alcohol service if the license is approved.
As for Paris West, which has specialized in high-quality American food in Cheyenne the last five years,
its owners said on Facebook
on Monday that they couldn’t be more excited for the future of their space. They will close Dec. 24, which will give Selimi time to prepare the restaurant for a January opening.
The council will still need to issue a license, however. Monday night’s hearing on the application was purely for public information; an official decision will come in the next few weeks.
The council did, however, approve licenses for a Bally’s location on Meadowland Drive and Wing Shack’s new location on Yellowstone.
While more discussion over the license is to come, Cheyenne Mayor Patrick Collins said he has enjoyed Selimi’s food before and looks forward to trying Fig and Olive for the first time when it opens.
