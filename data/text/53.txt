Cheyenne man federally indicted for alleged $270K wire fraud scheme
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A Cheyenne man is being federally indicted for reportedly defrauding three different victims out of a combined $270,000 in 2024. His trial will begin in January.
The United States Department of Justice announced that a grand jury has moved to indict Ariyan Devonte Trawick of Cheyenne for reported wire fraud and money laundering. According to the DOJ, he reportedly registered two businesses to open business accounts at two local banks in order to receive and launder illicit funds. He is considered innocent until being found guilty.
In June 2024, he reportedly convinced three separate individuals to wire a combined total of $270,000 to those accounts, which he reportedly attempted to transfer to beneficiaries in California and Hong Kong.
Trawick now faces four counts of wire fraud, one count of conspiracy to commit wire fraud and three counts of money laundering.
U.S. Attorney for the District of Wyoming Darin D. Smith announced the charges, while the Internal Revenue Service conducted the initial investigation.
Assistant U.S. Attorney Margaret Vierbuchen will prosecute the case before U.S. District Court Judge Alan Johnson at Trawick’s jury trial on Jan. 4 at 1:30 p.m.
