Retta Coleen Huston
Retta Coleen Huston:
1958 – 2026
Retta Huston, 68, of Cheyenne, passed at Davis Hospice Center surrounded by her loving family. She was born on February 23, 1958, in Kansas City, Kansas to her mother and father, Darrel Wise.
Retta was a home daycare provider and an active member of Cheyenne Church of Christ. She loved camping, shopping, and feeding her colony of feral cats.
She is survived by her husband, Lon Huston; her children: Melissa Gibson, Damon Huston, and Sierra Vigil; her eight grandchildren; and two great-grandchildren; her sisters: Ruby Walters, Donna Howard, and Gracie Castleberry.
Celebration of life services will be at Cheyenne Church of Christ at 10 a.m. Friday November 27.
In memory of Retta, we are requesting everyone wear purple in honor of her favorite color.
